# Business Documentation

These documents translate the Corex AgentOS technical roadmap into commercial,
investment, and partnership plans.

## Documents

- [Commercialization Strategy](commercialization-strategy.md) defines target
  customers, packaging, routes to market, validation gates, and commercial
  risks.
- [Competitive Landscape](competitive-landscape.md) compares Corex with the
  closest managed and open agent platforms using official product documentation.
- [Investor Brief](investor-brief.md) summarizes the opportunity, current
  status, defensibility, financing thesis, milestones, and diligence needs.
- [Metrics and Financial Model](metrics-and-financial-model.md) defines the
  evidence and formulas needed to build an auditable operating model without
  inventing projections.
- [Partner Program](partner-program.md) defines partner types, qualification,
  technical validation, benefits, obligations, and engagement flows.

## Document policy

These are internal planning and discussion documents. They do not constitute:

- an offer to sell securities or a solicitation to invest;
- audited financial statements or management-approved forecasts;
- a binding partner agreement, service level agreement, or price list;
- legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice;
- a commitment to deliver roadmap functionality on a fixed date.

Any externally shared version should be reviewed for confidentiality, factual
currency, trademark usage, licensing, securities compliance, and consistency
with approved company messaging.

## Evidence labels

Commercial claims should use one of these labels:

- **Observed:** supported by product telemetry, signed contracts, invoices, or
  documented customer evidence.
- **Validated:** supported by repeatable customer research or completed pilots.
- **Hypothesis:** plausible but not yet proven.
- **Target:** an internal objective, not a forecast or guarantee.

Do not present hypotheses or targets as observed traction.
